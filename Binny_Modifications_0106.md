# Client Meeting Modifications & Enhancements

## Inventory & Packaging

- Inventory manual upload for master cartons should not automatically add child box counts.
- If a master carton is unpacked, child box counts should not be added automatically.
- Users should manually add the required child boxes and print labels as needed.
- Provision to scan master cartons in e-commerce; all mapped child boxes should be auto-reflected.
- Sample inventory should be displayed separately.
- Samples are dispatched as a single foot instead of pairs; provision required to manage this process.

## User & Access Management

- Role Master module required to allow Admins to manage user access and permissions.

## Product & Inventory Management

- Dedicated E-commerce Inventory section.
- Provision to hide inactive products.
- Add comprehensive filters in the product listing section.
- Product upload via CSV should support up to 2,000 products in a single upload.
- Customer data bulk uploader in CSV format.

## Label Printing

- Support printing child box labels for up to 1,500 articles in a single operation.
- Reduce label font size to accommodate size suffixes such as 10K, 11K, etc., where 'K' denotes Kids sizes.

## Performance Optimization

- Optimize the Repack API, as rapid scanning is currently causing some child boxes to be skipped.

## Reports Required

### Inventory Reports
- Dead Stock Report
- Free & Unpacked Carton Report

### Sales & Dispatch Reports
- Most Selling and Least Selling Products (based on dispatch)
- Party-wise Most Selling and Least Selling Products
- Category-wise Most Selling and Least Selling Products
- Section-wise Most Selling and Least Selling Products

### Trend & Analytics Reports
- Monthly Trend Report for a Particular Article
- Monthly Purchase Pattern Report (Party-wise)
