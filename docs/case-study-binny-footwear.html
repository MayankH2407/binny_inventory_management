The Challenge

A warehouse running on handwriting.

Binny Footwear tracked inventory by writing article, colour, size, and quantity directly onto master cartons. But cartons get unpacked and repacked constantly — and nothing recorded those events. The records and the shelf drifted apart.

01

Inventory mismatches

Recorded stock stopped reflecting the physical stock on the shelf.

02

Phantom stock

Cartons that existed in the system held different — or fewer — items than expected.

03

Dispatch errors

Wrong products shipped, because no one could trust what was inside a carton.

04

No audit trail

When a discrepancy surfaced, there was no way to trace when or why contents changed.

The Solution

A digital hierarchy that mirrors the physical one.

Two levels of tracking, and a lifecycle the system enforces rather than merely records — so the digital record can never drift from reality.

Inner · Permanent

Child Box

One pair of footwear per box, carrying a permanent QR code that follows it from the production line all the way to dispatch.

1 pair = 1 identity
Outer · Dynamic

Master Carton

A temporary grouping container with a dynamic QR and a strict, enforced lifecycle. A child box lives in only one active carton at a time.
